
# 🚖 CityRide Analytics and Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

CityRide Analytics is a data-driven dashboard project designed to analyze ride-hailing operations across multiple cities. The dashboard provides actionable insights into trip performance, revenue trends, customer behavior, and service quality using interactive visualizations.
The project focuses on enabling **data-driven decision making** by identifying demand patterns, customer retention issues, and performance gaps.

## 📊 Key Highlights

* Analyzed **426K+ trips across 10 cities**
* Generated insights on **revenue (₹108M+)**, trip demand, and passenger behavior
* Built **interactive Power BI dashboard with 4 report pages**
* Tracked important KPIs like:

  * Total Trips
  * Revenue
  * Repeat Passenger Rate (RPR%)
  * Monthly Revenue Growth (MRG%)
  * Target vs Actual Performance

---

## 🧠 Business Problems Addressed

* How is trip demand distributed across cities?
* Which cities are performing well vs underperforming?
* Are revenue and trips meeting monthly targets?
* How strong is customer retention (repeat passengers)?
* What are the peak and declining revenue trends?
* Are service quality metrics (ratings) meeting expectations?

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Trip Metrics

* Total trips, weekday vs weekend trends
* City-wise trip distribution
* Monthly target vs actual comparison

### 2. Revenue Insights

* Total revenue and monthly revenue trends
* Revenue split by passenger type (new vs repeat)
* Average fare and revenue distribution

### 3. Passenger Analysis

* New vs repeat passengers
* Repeat Passenger Rate (RPR%)
* Customer segmentation and behavior patterns

### 4. Performance & Correlation

* Rating analysis (driver & passenger)
* Revenue growth (MRG%) trends
* Correlation between trips, revenue, and ratings

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* High demand observed in cities like **Jaipur and Lucknow**
* **Repeat customer rate is relatively low**, indicating retention gaps
* **Revenue growth declined in later months**, signaling performance issues
* Some cities show **below-target ratings**, highlighting service quality concerns
